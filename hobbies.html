My Hobbies & Interests

What I enjoy doing when I'm not studying or coding.

Photography

📷 Capturing Moments

Through the lens.

I got into photography when I got my first smartphone. I really enjoy street photography - capturing the everyday life of Dhaka. The colorful rickshaws, the busy streets, the golden hour light on old buildings in Puran Dhaka - there is always something interesting to photograph.

I mostly use my Canon EOS M50 but also shoot on my phone sometimes. Genres I like:

  • Street Photography
  • Landscape & Nature
  • Portraits
  • Architecture
